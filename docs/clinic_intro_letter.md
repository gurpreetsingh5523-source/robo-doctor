# Robo Doctor — Free Pilot Program for Your Clinic

**A letter to doctors and clinic administrators**

---

Dear Doctor,

My name is Gurpreet Singh. I have built **Robo Doctor**, a free,
offline-first healthcare assistant, as *seva* — selfless service for
communities that cannot easily afford hospital care. I am writing to invite
your clinic to join a **free 4-week pilot**.

## What Robo Doctor does for your clinic

- **Registers patients** (anonymously if needed) and **remembers every
  visit** — blood results, symptoms, analyses
- **Turns symptoms into a recommended test list** with urgency triage and
  red-flag warnings (e.g. chest pain + breathlessness = emergency)
- **Analyzes blood panels** against reference ranges and flags critical
  values instantly
- **Catches early trends** — e.g. fasting glucose creeping up across visits,
  before it becomes diabetes
- **Drafts prescriptions** for your review — *you approve, modify, or reject
  every single one; nothing reaches a patient without your signature*

## What it never does

- It never diagnoses or prescribes on its own. **You remain the doctor.**
  The system is an assistant with a perfect memory, nothing more.
- It never sends patient data anywhere. Everything stays on your clinic's
  computer.

## What the pilot involves

- 4 weeks, 50–100 patients, ~15 minutes of your time per week for feedback
- I set everything up personally, at no cost, and train your assistant in
  under an hour
- After 4 weeks we write a short summary together — yours to keep, whatever
  it says

## What I ask of you

Only your honest feedback — especially where the system is wrong. Every
draft you reject makes it safer for the next patient.
